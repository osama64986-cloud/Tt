# The Anime Universe (Java) — the mod we are bringing to Bedrock

Notes kept so they are never forgotten. Source: the mod's CurseForge and MCreator pages (seen through search; both sites
block automated reading) and its YouTube showcases.

- **Mod:** The Anime Universe by clozyfps, made with MCreator, Forge 1.16.5 / 1.18.2 (v2.0, Oct 2022), ~18k downloads.
- **Core idea:** you *battle anime characters as bosses and learn their abilities*, aiming to become the strongest
  "anime god". Bosses drop good loot. Some bosses have phases (beat Gon and Adult Gon appears).
- **Characters (mobs):** Goku, Naruto, Sasuke, Itachi, Gojo, Luffy, Tanjiro, Muzan, Gon, Killua, Kurapika, Hisoka,
  Meruem, Deku, Bakugo, Todoroki...
- **Powers as items:** Gojo's power, Kurapika's chains, Chidori, Rasengan, Shadow Clones, Sharingan, Rinnegan,
  All for One / One for All, Explosion and Ice quirks.
- **Extras:** travel to planets ("planetary beast"), 3D models, animations, sound effects, combined modes.

## What Anime Battles does with it
| The Anime Universe | Anime Battles (Bedrock) |
|---|---|
| Fight characters as bosses | `/scriptevent ab:boss goku` (or no name = random) — it comes for you |
| Boss phases (Gon -> Adult Gon) | Naruto Part I -> Shippuden -> War -> Adult, Sasuke likewise, Yuji -> Yuji (final) -> Sukuna, Tanjiro -> Demon Tanjiro |
| Learn their abilities | beating a character drops its **power item**; use it to cast that character's techniques (each use = the next one) |
| Become the strongest anime god | every *new* power learned raises your own power (60 + 18 per power) |
| Characters fighting | red vs blue teams, `/scriptevent ab:vs a b`, `/scriptevent ab:random` |
| Planets | not possible with Bedrock scripting (no custom dimensions) — not done |

Characters still to add from its roster: Itachi, Gon, Killua, Kurapika, Hisoka, Meruem, Deku, Bakugo, Todoroki.
