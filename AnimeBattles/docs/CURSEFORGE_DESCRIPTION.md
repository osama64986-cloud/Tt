# Anime Multiverse Clash (AMC)

**Nine anime worlds collide in Minecraft.** Put the legends of **Dragon Ball, Jujutsu Kaisen, One Piece, Naruto, Bleach, Demon Slayer, My Hero Academia, Hunter x Hunter and Chainsaw Man** into a red team and a blue team, then sit back and watch them fight.
Every character has their real transformations, signature techniques and fighting style.

## ⚔️ Features

- **43 anime fighters from 9 anime**, each with their full set of transformations, climbing one form at a time just like in the anime.
- **Smart fighters.** Every fighter reads the battle:
  - uses ranged attacks from afar and close-range techniques up close;
  - punishes openings, dodges big attacks and finishes weakened enemies;
  - retreats and heals when hurt.
  The stronger fighter usually wins, but a much smarter one can beat a slightly stronger opponent.
- **Legendary combat:**
  - beam struggles, fist clashes and guard breaks;
  - perfect counters and vanish-chases;
  - impact frames and heavy knockback that sends fighters sliding across the ground.
- **The world reacts.**
  - Hits tear trenches, craters form where fighters land, walls cave in and rocks fly.
  - The battlefield **rebuilds itself** after the fight.
- **Domain Expansions are real places:**
  - Gojo and Yuji raise a real dome of blocks around the fight.
  - Two domains can clash.
  - Sukuna's Malevolent Shrine rises behind him.
- **Giant summons:**
  - Susanoo in every stage: the fighter floats inside its chest.
  - Kurama, Gamabunta and Mahoraga.
  - Itachi's crows, Kakashi's ninja hounds, Rengoku's Flame Tiger, Killua's steel yo-yos and Megumi's black and white Divine Dogs.
  - Giant fists: Deku's Detroit Smash of green wind and Adult Gon's Rock of yellow nen are punched straight into the enemy; Ace hurls his Fire Fist of flame.
- **Cinematic camera** for the battle face-off, Domain Expansions, final transformations and the victory.
- **No text during fights.** Only the winning team is shown at the end.
- **Arena Builder:** one tap builds a complete, lit fighting arena anywhere:
  - a stone floor with team pads;
  - a wall with braziers and gates;
  - stands to watch from.
- **Team wars:** many fighters against many at once.

## 🎮 How to Play

When you join a world you get three items. The fighters' spawn eggs are in the creative inventory.

| Item | How to use |
|---|---|
| **Team Stick** | Hit a fighter to put it on the **red** team; the next one you hit goes to the **blue** team (it alternates). Use the stick to switch which team comes next. Sneak + use resets every team. |
| **Kill Stick** | Hit any mob to remove it instantly. Use it to turn block damage on or off. Sneak + use switches between full and low effects (for weaker phones). |
| **Arena Builder** | Hold to use: builds a full arena around you. If it gets wrecked, build a new one anywhere else. |

As soon as two fighters are on opposite teams, the battle begins.

### Commands
| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/scriptevent ab:vs goku vegeta` | Instant battle between two fighters (on the arena's team pads if you're in one) |
| `/scriptevent ab:random` | Random 1 vs 1 battle |
| `/scriptevent ab:war 4` | Team war: 4 vs 4 random fighters (2 to 6) |
| `/scriptevent ab:eggs` | Every fighter's spawn egg |
| `/scriptevent ab:kit` | The sticks and the Arena Builder again |
| `/scriptevent ab:griefing off` | Abilities don't break blocks |
| `/scriptevent ab:regen off` | Battle damage stays (the arena doesn't rebuild) |
| `/scriptevent ab:cinema off` | Turns off the cinematic camera |
| `/scriptevent ab:fx low` | Lighter effects for weaker devices |

Fighter names for `ab:vs`: goku, vegeta, broly, frieza, gojo, yuji, yuji_final, sukuna, luffy, zoro, naruto_kid, naruto_teen, naruto_war, naruto_adult, sasuke_kid, sasuke, sasuke_war, sasuke_adult, itachi, kakashi, madara, ichigo, tanjiro, tanjiro_demon, muzan, deku, killua, denji, rengoku, ace, gohan, bakugo, gon, akaza, megumi, aizen, byakuya, power, makima, allmight, todoroki, hisoka, kurapika.

## 📦 Installation

1. Download **AnimeMultiverseClash.mcaddon** and open it. Minecraft imports both packs automatically.
2. Create a world (or edit one) and turn on **both** the Behavior Pack and the Resource Pack.
3. Requires **Minecraft Bedrock 1.26.50 or newer**. Works on phone, PC and console.

When updating: if Minecraft says the pack is already installed, delete the old packs in **Settings → Storage**, then import the new file.

## ⚠️ Notes

- The Arena Builder clears the space where it builds (trees, hills and other blocks), so build it where you want it.
- Big battles with many fighters can be heavy on weaker phones. Use `/scriptevent ab:fx low` if needed.
