# Combat design notes (research, kept so it is never forgotten)

Research on the best-known anime combat mods for Minecraft Java (Dragon Block C and its successor Dragon Ball: Re,
Jujutsu Craft / Sorcery Fight and its addons, Epic Fight) — the mechanics that make their fights feel legendary:

| Mechanic | Where it comes from | How Anime Battles does it |
|---|---|---|
| Perfect Dodge + Perfect Counter (slow-time window, teleport behind the enemy) | Dragon Ball: Re | dodge sidestep, then often a vanish behind the attacker and a counter blow |
| Ki Guard / Perfect Guard (block, timed parry) | Dragon Ball: Re | guard stance (arms crossed) cuts damage to 35%; a far stronger attacker breaks the guard and stuns |
| Black Flash builds over consecutive hits, custom particles, sounds, screen shake | Limitless / Jujutsu Craft addons | combos, Black Flash moves, screen shake on heavy hits |
| Impact & stun on heavy weapon hits | Epic Fight | impact frame: both freeze for an instant, white flash, camera shake, target staggered |
| Domain clash won by the stronger side | Limitless | domain clash already decided by power |
| Ki struggle / beam clash | Dragon Block C style | beam clash already: the stronger pushes the collision point onto the weaker |
| Charged ki attacks, warp Kamehameha | Dragon Ball: Re | charged orbs, Instant Kamehameha |

Extra rules from the player (must always hold):
- No text on screen during fights: no ability names, no lines, no counters. Only the winning team at the end.
- Hits push targets back ~7 blocks along the ground — never flying into the air.
- Transformations climb step by step, never straight to the last form.
- The stronger fighter always wins; Goku stays the strongest. Fights must last (high toughness).
- Health stays <= 1000 (higher values may break the entity in Bedrock); durability comes from toughness.
