# Anime Multiverse — Characters

**Dragon Ball**
- **Goku** — The strongest fighter in the mod. Climbs from base form through Super Saiyan, Super Saiyan 3, God, Blue and finally Ultra Instinct, where his body dodges and counters on its own.
- **Vegeta** — The proud Saiyan Prince. Galick Gun, Big Bang Attack and Final Flash, all the way up to Ultra Ego, where every blow he takes makes him stronger.
- **Broly** — The Legendary Super Saiyan. The angrier he gets the stronger he becomes, an unstoppable rage that can overpower Vegeta.
- **Frieza** — The Emperor of the Universe. Death Beams, the Death Ball and telekinesis, then Golden Frieza.

**Jujutsu Kaisen**
- **Gojo Satoru** — The honoured one. Infinity stops every attack. He uses Blue, Red and Hollow Purple, and his Domain Expansion, Unlimited Void, raises a real dome around the fight.
- **Yuji Itadori** — The vessel with superhuman strength. Divergent Fist and Black Flash; land one and he enters "the zone".
- **Yuji Itadori (Final)** — Shinjuku Yuji, a master of Black Flash with Reverse Cursed Technique and his own Domain Expansion.
- **Ryomen Sukuna** — The King of Curses. Dismantle, Cleave, the Mahoraga wheel, and Malevolent Shrine, which rises behind him as he slices everything in range.

**One Piece**
- **Monkey D. Luffy** — Every Gear from 2 to 5. Jet Pistol, Gear 4's Kong Gun, and Gear 5 where he turns the battlefield into a cartoon.
- **Roronoa Zoro** — The Three-Sword Style. Oni Giri, Tatsumaki, the nine-sword Asura demon, and King of Hell Three Thousand Worlds.

**Naruto**
- **Naruto (Kid)** — Shadow Clones, the Rasengan and the first red chakra of the Nine-Tails.
- **Naruto (Shippuden)** — Sage Mode, the Rasenshuriken, and the dangerous Four- and Eight-Tails forms.
- **Naruto (War)** — Kurama Chakra Mode, Tailed Beast Bombs and Six Paths Sage Mode with the Truth-Seeking Balls.
- **Naruto (Hokage)** — The Seventh Hokage, up to Baryon Mode, his last and most powerful form.
- **Sasuke (Kid)** — Chidori and the Cursed Seal, with its wings at Level 2.
- **Sasuke (Shippuden)** — Kirin, Amaterasu and his first Susanoo.
- **Sasuke (War)** — Eternal Mangekyo, Perfect Susanoo and the Rinnegan, finishing with Indra's Arrow.
- **Sasuke (Adult)** — The shadow Hokage. Amenotejikara, the Rinnegan's genjutsu and a full Susanoo.
- **Itachi Uchiha** — The genius of the Uchiha clan. Fire Style, a flock of real crows, Tsukuyomi, Amaterasu, and a red Susanoo with the sealing Totsuka Blade.
- **Kakashi Hatake** — The Copy Ninja. He charges in with Raikiri, sends his ninja hounds out of the ground, warps targets with Kamui, and copies his opponent's own technique back at them.
- **Madara Uchiha** — The legend reborn. Edo Tensei, a blue Perfect Susanoo, then Six Paths with the Truth-Seeking Balls.

**Bleach**
- **Ichigo Kurosaki** — Shikai, Fullbring, Bankai, the Hollow mask, Vasto Lorde, the Final Getsuga Tensho and True Bankai.

**Demon Slayer**
- **Tanjiro Kamado** — Water Breathing, then Hinokami Kagura, the dance of the Sun.
- **Demon Tanjiro** — Turned by Muzan, with flesh tendrils bursting from his back and the Demon King's power.
- **Muzan Kibutsuji** — The Demon King. His disguise falls away to reveal his final form of whips, mouths and regeneration.

Every fighter is smart: they choose their technique for the moment, punish openings, dodge, retreat and heal. The
stronger fighter usually wins, but a much smarter one can beat a slightly stronger opponent.
